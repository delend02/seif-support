Support

For questions about Seif, email contact@leikuruab.com.

What to include

Tell us your Seif version, device model, operating system version and the steps that led to the issue. Include a screenshot only if it contains no private information.

Never send your master password, vault files, passwords or two-factor secrets.

Lost master password

Seif has no account recovery service. We cannot decrypt your vault or recover a lost master password. An encrypted backup also requires the password that protected it when it was created.

Quick unlock

You can enable biometric quick unlock in Settings. If it is unavailable, enter your master password to open the vault.

Backups

Export an encrypted backup from Settings and store it somewhere you control. Earlier backups remain separate copies when entries are changed or deleted.
