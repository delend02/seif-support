Privacy policy

Seif is a local password manager operated by LEIKUR, UAB, Gedimino pr. 20, LT-01103 Vilnius, Lithuania. Privacy questions: contact@leikuruab.com.

Your vault stays on your device

Passwords, notes, folders, two-factor secrets and password history are saved in an encrypted vault on your device. Seif has no account service, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not send your vault contents to us.

Quick unlock

Quick unlock is optional and stores unlock material using the device's protected key storage. Seif never receives your fingerprint or face data. Turning quick unlock off removes its stored unlock key.

Clipboard and files you choose

Copying places the selected value in the system clipboard. Seif requests a 30-second expiry on iOS and clears its own copy when possible on Android. Other apps can receive a value when you paste it.

You choose where encrypted backups are exported. A cloud storage provider you select handles the file under its own privacy terms. CSV imports are processed on your device; CSV files are not encrypted by Seif, so remove them when you no longer need them.

Deletion and backups

Permanently deleted entries are removed from the current vault. Existing exported backups are separate copies and are not changed. You control these files through the operating system. We cannot recover a lost master password.

Support and system services

If you email support, we use your email and message to respond. Do not include passwords, vault exports or two-factor secrets. Apple and your operating system may process optional system diagnostics under your device settings.
